# Profile button interactions

## Agreed scope

- Add hover and press animations to the Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok links and the light/dark toggle.
- Social links should have a gentle lift, arrow movement, and clear tap feedback.
- All four controls should feel consistent and smooth.

## Confirmed behavior

- On a mouse or trackpad, controls lift 2px on hover and social arrows move 2px upward and right.
- Pressing a control scales it to 98% and changes its colors for immediate feedback on phones as well.
- Switching themes briefly rotates the sun/moon icon.
- Social links open immediately; animations do not delay navigation.
- Reduced-motion preferences disable movement and transitions while retaining color feedback.
